Digital / Marketing Specialist

Help Game API communicate its products, opportunities and developer ecosystem to a growing digital audience.

Remote / Nigeria Contract / Part-time Marketing

About the role

You will help grow awareness of Game API through digital campaigns, content, social channels and community engagement. The ideal candidate can combine creativity with measurable marketing execution.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and create digital marketing campaigns.
  • Develop social media and promotional content.
  • Help communicate products, developer opportunities and partnerships.
  • Track campaign performance and suggest improvements.
  • Research audiences, trends and relevant communities.

Requirements

  • Experience with digital marketing, content or social media.
  • Strong written communication and creative thinking.
  • Basic understanding of analytics and campaign performance.
  • Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
  • A portfolio of campaigns, content or previous work is an advantage.

What success looks like

Success means helping more developers and potential partners discover Game API, understand its value and engage with the platform through useful and authentic communication.

Show us what you can create. Links to campaigns, social pages, articles, designs or other relevant work are welcome.
